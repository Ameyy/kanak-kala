# Kanak Jewellery (कनक कला) — Luxury Editorial Website

An artisanal, minimalist, and royal e-commerce website designed for the heritage jewellery brand **Kanak Jewellery** (`@_kanak_kala`), inspired by high-jewelry editorial maisons (Terona / Maison Lumière aesthetic).

---

## ✦ Design Architecture & Editorial Inspirations

1. **Editorial Typography (Mixed Roman & Italic Serif)**
   - Headings set in **Cormorant Garamond** with elegant italic accent words:
     - `HERITAGE, *RENDERED* ETERNAL.`
     - `Four houses of *craft*.`
     - `A legacy of quiet *devotion* to craft.`
     - `Coveted by *our patrons*.`
     - `Letters from the *atelier*.`

2. **The Iconic Jharokha (Temple Arch) Feature**
   - Central scalloped temple arch framing signature jewellery pieces.
   - Flanked by floating quality metrics: `Premium Gold Finish`, `Hand-Set Stones`, `Traditional Craft`, `4.9★ Customer Rating`.

3. **Hairline Grid Columns ("Four Houses of Craft")**
   - 4 vertical columns with hairline dividers displaying:
     * Peshwai Naths
     * Masoli Karnaful
     * Bridal Bangles
     * Panna & Kundan
   - Pill-shaped call-to-action buttons with subtle directional arrows (`→`).

4. **Asymmetric Atelier Spotlight**
   - Left: Grand arched showcase displaying the Padmarag Nath.
   - Right: Staggered product cards with quick exploration links.

5. **Curated Clientele Edition**
   - 4-column minimal product cards with wishlist heart toggles, direct links to individual product pages, price, and instant "Add to Bag".

6. **Instagram & Atelier Strip**
   - Clean centered `@_kanak_kala` headline above a seamless 5-tile photograph showcase.

7. **Patron Testimonials & Atelier Newsletter**
   - Editorial testimonial quotes from patrons across Maharashtra (Pune, Mumbai, Kolhapur).
   - Solid deep-emerald card for "Letters from the Atelier".

## ✦ Color System: Strict 60-30-10 Percentile Rule

Based on your provided emerald-and-gold swatches, arranged in high-fashion editorial balance:

| Ratio | Role | Hex Code | Visual Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| **60%** | **Dominant Base** | `#F6F1E8` | Luxury champagne parchment canvas & atmosphere |
| **30%** | **Secondary Structure** | `#08372A` | Deep royal emerald (headers, primary typography, hero, cards, borders) |
| **10%** | **Imperial Accent** | `#E3B43C` | Regal gold (CTA buttons, italic text accents, price markers, active states) |

- **Zero Gradients**: 100% solid, elegant, high-luxury tones.
- **Strictly 3 Colors**: `#F6F1E8`, `#08372A`, `#E3B43C`.
